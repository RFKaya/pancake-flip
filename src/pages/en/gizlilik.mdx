# 🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

## 1. Data collected

Pancake Flip! does **not store or send** your personal data to any external server. Your game progress (stars, coins, upgrades, receipt codes) and preferences (theme, sound, vibration) are kept only in your device's local storage (`localStorage`).

## 2. Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, ads or tracking tools. The contact form is a course example; what you type is not sent anywhere.

## 3. Data protection notice (KVKK)

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the app does not process personal data on any server, so no personal data is collected by a data controller. You are the only one in control of the data on your device.

## 4. Deleting your data

The "reset save" option on the profile screen, or uninstalling the app, deletes all game data on the device.
