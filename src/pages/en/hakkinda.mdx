# 🥞 About

**Pancake Flip!** is a portrait mobile game where you prepare customers' orders in a small pancake shop against the clock. Cook the pancake in the pan, flip it at the right moment, add fillings and sauce, top it off and serve the plate. The customer waits, the pancake burns: flip, stack, hurry!

<KrepCevir client:load dil="en" />

## How to play

- Flip the pancake when it is ready; flip too early and it is raw, too late and it burns.
- Stack pancakes, fillings and sauces on the plate in the order the ticket shows.
- Serve before the customer's patience runs out. A PERFECT serve earns combo and tips.
